GPS Overview

What is GPS?

Two Navigators, one set of student-success data.

Administrator Navigator. Administrator Navigator gives district and school administrators a holistic view of the metrics that drive student success, in one platform: over 20 dashboards, curated and designed to fit within an existing workflow. It provides high-level overviews while also diving into subgroup and student-level data.

Teacher Navigator. Teacher Navigator is the GPS ecosystem's classroom-facing view of student attendance, assessments, and well-being. It organizes everything into three nested levels so you can start with a whole grade and drill down to a single student.

Who is it for?

District and school staff.

Why use it?

Your data, ready when you need it.

  • A data-informed look at your students, from a district view, school view, down to a single student.
  • An immediate, accessable resource for teachers and administrators.
  • A free holistic comprehensive view of student data in your district.

How do I get started?

Getting into the Navigators.

  1. Go to the EA Portal. To log into any of the GPS platforms, your starting place is always the EA Portal, the hub where all GPS products live. Open the EA Portal →
  2. Log in with single sign-on (SSO). Click the login button. If you work in a school or a school district, log in with Classlink. If you are a state employee working for SCDE, or you do not work for a school district or school, log in with Microsoft Azure. Each user has their own login settings, so this may differ for you.
  3. Select your application. The portal shows every application you have been granted access to. Select Admin Nav or Teacher Nav. To open a platform again, log in to the EA Portal, select your application, and start working in the data.

If you run into issues logging in or need access, reach out to your district user administrator. For help understanding the dashboards, or for suggestions or questions, email gps@ed.sc.gov. In the EA Portal, the drop-down next to your initials has Help Guides and the EA Support Portal, where you can make a ticket.
