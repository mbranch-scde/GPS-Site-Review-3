Administrator Navigator

A view across schools and students.

Home for 20+ comprehensive dashboards covering a high-level view of assessments, course grades, college and career readiness, and predictions and targets, and more. Every chart, table, and visualization is available to be exported.

What is it?

Administrator Navigator gives district and school administrators a holistic view of the metrics that drive student success, in one platform: over 20 dashboards, curated and designed to fit within an existing workflow.

It provides high-level overviews while also diving into subgroup and student-level data. The highlighted dashboards each drill from a district-wide roll-up down to a single student.

Who is it for?

  • District and school administrators
  • Principals and school leaders
  • District accountability staff and data coordinators
  • District finance and data teams
  • PowerSchool administrators
  • Counselors

How to access

  1. Open the EA Portal.
  2. If you work in a school or school district, log in with Classlink. If you are a state employee working for SCDE, log in with Microsoft Azure.
  3. Select Admin Nav.

Need access? Reach out to your district user administrator, or use the request form.

Start with a question

Step-by-step instructions for common questions.

How do I understand what percent of my students are proficient in ELA or Math?

  1. Open Administrator Navigator.
  2. In the left menu, expand Accountability and select Metrics Dashboard. The dashboard shows proficiency percentages, the % of students that pass certain exam thresholds.
  3. Set filters and apply: School Year, Metric Category, District/School Level Metric, then Apply Filters.
  4. Move through the three views: District, School, Student, to zoom from roll-up to roster.
Metrics Dashboard one-pager →

How do I learn how my students are progressing against their growth targets?

  1. Open Administrator Navigator. In the left menu, select Predictions and Targets (just below Growth).
  2. Set filters and apply: School Year, School, Target Type, Subject, Prediction Type, then Apply Filters.
  3. Read the headline: the Percent Predicted card and the by-target-type and by-prior-proficiency charts.
  4. To build a watch-list, open the student table, filter to Predicted to Meet Target = "no", and export via the three-dot menu.
  5. Percent Met is empty until summatives arrive. Before the current year's SC READY summative, the Percent Met card shows No data.
Predictions and Targets one-pager →

How did students perform on assessments?

  1. Open the Assessment dashboard in Administrator Navigator.
  2. Set the year. Clear all first if results look wrong.
  3. Select assessment and subject. Keep the test definition consistent.
  4. Select grade. Cut scores often differ by grade.
  5. Select season(s) to compare like administrations and see trends over time.
  6. Add school or student group. Narrow only after the base slice is valid.

More questions GPS can help answer

  • Which subgroups need the most support to be on track to graduate? On Track and College and Career Readiness Dashboard
  • How is my school doing in a certain metric compared to other schools in my district? Metric Comparison
  • What percentage of district students scored a B or higher in English 2, Semester 1? Course Grades: Snapshot
  • Which sections have the highest chronic absence? Sections Dashboard
