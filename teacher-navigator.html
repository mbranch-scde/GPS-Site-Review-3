What is it?

Teacher Navigator is the GPS ecosystem's classroom-facing view of student attendance, assessments, and well-being. It organizes everything into three nested levels so you can start with a whole grade and drill down to a single student.

  • Grade: a whole grade at a school. Spot cohort-wide patterns.
  • Classroom: one course section. Compare your class to the district average.
  • Student: one child's full picture, including programs and CCR.

Who is it for?

  • Teachers
  • Counselors
  • Instructional coaches
  • Principals and other school and district administrators
  • MTSS coordinators and interventionists

What you see is based on your access. If you are missing a grade, class, or student, it is usually an access or roster question. Route it to your school's GPS contact.

How to access

  1. Open the EA Portal.
  2. If you work in a school or school district, log in with Classlink.
  3. Select Teacher Nav.

Need access? Reach out to your district user administrator, or use the request form.

Start with a question

Step-by-step instructions for common questions.

How did Grade 5 perform on previous assessments? Who needs more help before moving on to 6th grade?

  1. Click Teacher Navigator. You open to your grade levels.
  2. Use the left rail to move: find a… (search), grade, class, student, group.
  3. Open a level and pick a tab, such as assessments.
  4. Go wide to narrow. Grade: is there a pattern across the cohort? Classroom: compare this class to the district average. Student: check subscores for which skills, and performance over time for the trend.
Getting-started one-pager →

How are my students predicted to grow?

  1. Click Teacher Navigator and select a school or class, then select a student.
  2. Open the Performance over Time tab. It is available at the student level only.
  3. Performance over time shows predicted AVT based on historical assessments.
  4. State equivalent puts different tests on one yardstick. It is the scale the performance over time chart uses.
Getting-started one-pager →

How do I create a group of students for an intervention?

  1. Click the group icon in the left sidebar, alongside find a…, grade, class, and student.
  2. Click + add group. Name it, and pick school or district scope.
  3. Add students and staff on the two tabs. Filter by name, classroom, grade, or school, check, then add to group.
  4. Click view group. The group's data view has four tabs: at a glance, updates, assessments, and well-being.
  5. Open Updates and click + Add New under interventions. Enter a name, description, content area, and start and end dates.
Groups and interventions one-pager →

FERPA caution: Adding a staff member to a group gives them student-level access to the students in it. Granting school or district admin rights gives them access to every student in that scope, not just the group.
