Guided TourAverage Daily Membership (ADM)

ADM is the foundational enrollment count for South Carolina school funding — the number that drives State Aid to Classrooms allocations and Add-on Weights (AOW) for the year. This guided tour shows you how to find the dashboard, read your district's totals at the four reporting milestones, drill down through schools, EFA codes, grades, and students, and — critically — how to preserve point-in-time snapshots before the data keeps updating past your reporting day.

Orientation

What it is & why use it

The Average Daily Membership (ADM) Dashboard surfaces your district's enrollment count using the rules and exclusions that drive the State Aid to Classrooms and Add-on Weights (AOW) calculations. ADM isn't just "how many students are enrolled" — it's the number SCDE uses to allocate dollars per pupil, weighted by funded subgroups (poverty, English-learner status, students with disabilities, etc.).

District lens

For finance and accountability teams, this is the dashboard that lets you verify State Aid to Classrooms and AOW counts before funding cycles lock in. If your ADM at Day 45 is off by 50 students, that's tens of thousands of dollars in funding implications. The dashboard pulls from the same Ed-Fi data flowing into SCDE — so any correction made in PowerSchool flows through in roughly a day.

Principal lens

For school leaders, ADM is the number your district uses to allocate resources back to your building. The School Report panel lets you see your own ADM by grade and EFA code — so you can confirm what your district will see when they roll up. If you're enrolling students whose AOW categories aren't being captured (e.g., a student qualifies for Academic Assistance but isn't tagged), that's a finding worth surfacing.

Quick definitions

ADM (Average Daily Membership): the average number of students enrolled per day across a reporting period. Not just "students enrolled today."

State Aid to Classrooms: South Carolina's state per-pupil funding for districts, allocated using ADM. It replaced the Education Finance Act (EFA) funding model.

A note on labels: the dashboard still uses "EFA" in its field names (EFA ADM, EFA Total Days, EFA Group, EFA Code). Those fields are the State Aid to Classrooms counts.

AOW (Add-on Weights): additional funding tied to specific student characteristics (e.g., poverty, EL, gifted) on top of the State Aid to Classrooms base.

Go deeperWhy ADM, not raw enrollment?

Raw enrollment moves day to day — students transfer in, transfer out, enroll late, get withdrawn. ADM smooths that volatility by averaging across a reporting period. Two students who each enrolled for half the year contribute the same to ADM as one student enrolled the whole year. That's the right measure for funding, but it means the dashboard's headline number won't match a single-day roster pull from PowerSchool.

Go deeperHow fast do corrections propagate?

Per EA's October 13, 2025 office hours: corrections made in PowerSchool flow through to the ADM dashboard within roughly a day — same cadence as the rest of Administrator Navigator. Aldo specifically pointed out that the dashboard's data freshness "should match the Average Daily Attendance date" — so if you want to confirm the latest sync, check the ADA dashboard or the data-freshness indicator on any sister dashboard.

Cadence

The four reporting days

ADM is calculated at four standard reporting milestones across the school year. The Day of School Year filter is required — every view of the dashboard reflects the milestone you pick. The dashboard is calendar-aware: it knows each district's specific 45th and 135th days, so you don't need to translate calendar dates yourself.

5days
Early sanity check
First snapshot after school opens. Useful for catching gross enrollment errors before they compound.
45days
Primary funding anchor
The headline milestone. State Aid to Classrooms allocations key off this number — the one most districts spend time validating.
90days
Mid-year check
Roughly halfway through the year. A trend check between the two main reporting days.
135days
Second major reporting
The second standard reporting day. Used alongside Day 45 for the comparison columns EA is adding.

EA announced columns to compare current-year values with the previous year's 45-day and 135-day numbers — targeted for release within two weeks of the October 13 session. That should be live in the production dashboard now. If you don't see comparison columns yet, refresh and re-check, or submit a ticket.

4
Funding model

State Aid to Classrooms vs Add-on Weights

The dashboard surfaces two parallel calculations side by side — the State Aid to Classrooms base, and the weighted AOW totals. They answer different questions and they're funded differently.

State Aid to Classrooms

The base per-pupil count

Counts each student in your district with one weight, regardless of their characteristics. It's the foundation of the state's per-pupil funding formula. You'll see EFA Total Days (sum of every enrolled-day) and EFA ADM (the average per day). The dashboard still labels these with "EFA."

AOW · Add-on Weights

The funded subgroups, weighted

Layered on top of State Aid to Classrooms. Students get additional weighted counts based on funded categories (Academic Assistance, Limited English Proficient, Pupils in Poverty, etc.). AOW Total Days and AOW ADM are the weighted totals — drives the supplementary funding districts receive.

Reading the four stat cards

At the top of every populated view you'll see four stat cards: EFA Total Days, EFA ADM, AOW Total Days, AOW ADM. The "Total Days" cards are the sum of every student-day; the "ADM" cards are those totals divided by the number of days in the reporting period — i.e., the average per day. Compare Day 5 to Day 45 and the ADM numbers stay roughly stable (they're averages); the Total Days numbers grow proportionally.

Go deeperWhy the AOW number is so much smaller than State Aid to Classrooms

In the worked example, EFA ADM is about 75,217 while AOW ADM is about 49,984. That's not because there are fewer students — it's because only students who qualify for an AOW category contribute. AOW is a count of weighted instances; if a student doesn't qualify for any funded weight, they don't contribute to the AOW totals at all. A student who qualifies for two add-ons contributes more than one. Different denominators, different ratios.

If a panel looks empty, scan the bar first to make sure your filters aren't over-narrowed.

6
Worked example

Run your first analysis

Let's walk through the analysis Aldo demonstrated in the October 13 session — verifying the district ADM at Day 45 and drilling down to a student-level check.

The question

What is our district's ADM at Day 45 — both State Aid to Classrooms and AOW — and does the school-level breakdown line up?

Set your filters

  1. School Year: 2025-26
  2. LEA Name: your district
  3. Day of School Year: 45
  4. Leave EFA Group, AOW Group, and Student Name blank for the first pass.
  5. Click Apply filters at the bottom of the filter panel.

A · Read the District Metrics

Scroll to the top. The four stat cards give you your headline. In the worked example, EFA ADM = 75,215.04 and AOW ADM = 49,982.80. Those are your "is the funding-eligible enrollment number sane?" gut-check values.

B · Drill into the District Report

Just below the metrics is the District Report table — tabbed between EFA ADM, AOW ADM, and District Comparison Days. Use this to see day-by-day or EFA-code-by-EFA-code breakdowns. The Comparison Days tab is where the new year-over-year comparison columns land.

C · Drop to School View

Keep scrolling. The School Report panel shows the same metrics broken out per school. If your district total looks off, this is where you find the school(s) driving the anomaly.

D · Drill to the Student Membership Report

The last panel is the roster. Filter by School Name (or use the in-table search) to isolate a specific school, then scan the student-level rows. This is the panel you'd export when you need to hand a registrar an audit list.

District lens · suggested next moves
  • Sort the School Report by EFA ADM descending. Your top schools should match your largest buildings.
  • Filter AOW Group to Pupils in Poverty and check whether your free-and-reduced eligibility uploads are flowing through.
  • Compare Day 45 EFA ADM with the same district's number in your finance office's records. Any gap above a few students is worth investigating.
Principal lens · suggested next moves
  • Filter the School Report to just your school. Confirm the EFA ADM matches what you expected based on opening enrollment.
  • Open the Student Membership Report and skim for surprises — students you don't recognize on your roster, or expected students who aren't there.
  • If your AOW count looks low, check whether students who should qualify for Academic Assistance / EL / disability weights have the right flags set in PowerSchool.
Go deeperThe 5-level drill-down hierarchy

Per Aldo in the October 13 session, ADM lets you drill District → School → EFA code → Grade → Student. That's one level deeper than Metrics. The EFA code drill is the unique one — it lets you isolate, for example, "show me only my Limited English Proficient counts across schools" without breaking the rest of your view.

7
Preserve

Export & preserve

Export the views you need for sharing with finance, for record-keeping, or for archive. Every panel supports CSV and Excel export from the three-dot menu in the upper-right of the panel.

  1. Open the panel you want to export (District Report, School Report, or Student Membership Report).
  2. Click the three-dot menu (⋮) in the panel's upper-right corner.
  3. Choose Export to CSV or Export to Excel. The file downloads to your computer.

The most important caveat: ADM data does NOT freeze

Per Aldo, October 13 office hours: the dashboard continues to update even after a reporting window closes. That means if you check Day 45 ADM on the actual 45th day and then check it again two months later, the numbers can have shifted because corrections kept flowing in.

If you need a point-in-time snapshot — for finance, for audit, for a board memo — export the extract on the reporting day and save it as your authoritative record. The dashboard is not your archive; your exported CSV/Excel is.

Go deeperFERPA reminder on student-level exports

The Student Membership Report contains student-level data and is covered by FERPA. Treat the export like any other student information system extract: keep it on district-managed storage, avoid emailing it externally, and delete it when the audit cycle ends. District and School-level exports don't contain student-level data and can be shared more freely.

From the field

FAQ — drawn from EA office hours, 2025-10-13

Questions from real users in Aldo's ADM office hours session, paraphrased and grouped for reference.

FAQWhich year do I pick — "2025" or "2026"?

Nichole raised this in the session because the year filter formatting was inconsistent with other dashboards. EA committed to standardizing the format to "25-26" / "24-25" style across the ADM and Metrics dashboards. If you're still seeing single-year labels, use the higher year (2026 for the 2025-26 school year).

FAQHow do I know the dashboard data is current?

Per Aldo, ADM dashboard data freshness should match the Average Daily Attendance date — so check the ADA dashboard (or any sister dashboard with a visible sync timestamp) to confirm the latest refresh. Corrections in PowerSchool typically propagate within a day.

FAQDoes the data freeze after the 45th day passes?

No. The dashboard continues to update past every reporting day. That's a feature for ongoing data quality work, but it means your "Day 45" view today might show slightly different numbers than your "Day 45" view three weeks ago. If you need to preserve the value on the actual reporting day, export the CSV/Excel that day and keep it as your point-in-time record.

FAQDoes the dashboard know each district's specific 45th day?

Yes. Aldo confirmed the system accounts for each district's actual calendar — your Day 45 is your Day 45, not a generic state-wide September date. The dashboard handles the calendar math for you.

FAQIs there a 180-day filter?

Not yet. Melissa asked about this in the October 13 session — she pointed out that financial audits sometimes need a 180-day final-snapshot value. EA acknowledged the request and said they'd consider adding it. If a 180-day milestone matters for your audits, submit a ticket at support.edanalytics.org to add weight to the request.

FAQHow do I compare ADM to PowerSchool's own count?

EA mentioned a recommended workflow in the October 13 session for comparing PowerSchool's enrollment reports against ADM. The two won't match exactly — ADM applies the State Aid to Classrooms inclusion / exclusion rules, while PowerSchool's basic enrollment counts everyone. The expected pattern: PowerSchool count ≥ EFA Total Days. If EFA Total Days shows more than PowerSchool, something is wrong with the State Aid to Classrooms rule application — submit a ticket.
